## Summary

Design the courses, quizzes and evaluations that students take on StudyVibe: write clear lessons, build question banks that measure what was taught, and help teachers turn their syllabus into assessments that are fair and automatically marked.

## About the role

On StudyVibe a course is made of chapters and lessons (text, video and PDF documents), each lesson can end with a quiz, teachers can set assignments, and they can run evaluations, live or at the student's own pace, whose multiple-choice questions are marked automatically. Questions come from question banks that teachers write and import into the platform in CSV, JSON or ZIP files with images, and each question can carry an explanation that students see when they review their result.

The education team makes sure that what is published on the platform is of high quality: that a lesson teaches what it says it teaches, that a question has one correct answer and no trick, and that an evaluation really measures the learning objectives of the course. You work with teachers at partner institutions and with our product team.

Your work covers four things. You design course structures with teachers. You write and review lessons and questions. You analyse the results of evaluations to find questions that do not work (too easy, too hard, or misleading) and you fix them. And you write the guides and run the training sessions that help teachers do this well on their own. You do not need to write code: you work in the platform, in the import file formats, and in spreadsheets.

After six months you are expected to have reviewed the question banks of the courses assigned to you against our quality checklist, trained the teachers of those courses, and reduced the number of questions that students contest because they were unclear.

This is a hybrid position based in Douala. You work at least three days a week from our Douala office and the other days from home, and you visit partner institutions for workshops from time to time. The contract is full-time.

## Responsibilities

- Meet teachers to turn a syllabus into a course structure of chapters, lessons and learning objectives written as outcomes that can be observed.
- Write and edit lesson content in clear French or English, designed to be read on a phone as well as on a computer.
- Write multiple-choice questions with one unambiguous correct answer, plausible wrong answers and a short explanation shown at correction.
- Review question banks written by teachers against a written quality checklist, and return them with precise comments.
- Build evaluations: choose the questions, set the time per question and the balance of difficulty, and check the evaluation against the learning objectives before it is scheduled.
- Prepare question banks in the platform's import formats (CSV, JSON, or a ZIP package with images) and test the import before it reaches students.
- Analyse the results of each evaluation to find questions that are too easy, too hard or misleading, and fix or retire them.
- Write and maintain the content standards guide, the templates and the examples that teachers use.
- Run training sessions, in person and online, for teachers on writing questions and building courses.
- Describe to the product and engineering teams what teachers need, and test new features before they are released.

## Requirements

- At least 3 years of experience in instructional design, curriculum development, teaching or training, during which you wrote course material and assessments.
- Proven experience writing assessment questions (multiple-choice or other types); you can show examples in your application.
- A clear understanding of learning objectives and of how to write them, for example with Bloom's taxonomy or a similar framework.
- Excellent written French and English, with strong grammar and the ability to explain a difficult idea simply.
- Experience with an online learning platform or learning management system (for example Moodle or a similar tool), as a teacher, designer or administrator.
- Comfort with spreadsheets: sorting, filtering, formulas such as averages and percentages, and simple charts.
- Rigour: you check facts, answers and spelling, and you never publish a question that you have not tested.
- A bachelor's degree in education, in a subject that you teach, or in a related field.
- The right to work in Cameroon, and the ability to work from our Douala office at least three days a week.

## Nice to have

- A master's degree in education, instructional design or a related field.
- Knowledge of psychometrics: item difficulty and discrimination.
- Experience writing video lesson scripts.
- Experience writing mathematical or scientific content with LaTeX or equation notation.
- Knowledge of accessible content (WCAG).
- Experience teaching in higher education in Cameroon or in the region.
- Experience training adult professionals.
