## Summary

Win universities and training centres in Cameroon and the wider region as new StudyVibe customers: find them, run the demonstration, negotiate the agreement, and hand them over to onboarding with a signed contract and a clear plan.

## About the role

StudyVibe is sold to institutions, not to individual students: universities, faculties, higher-education schools and professional training centres that want to run courses, assignments and evaluations online and give their students certificates that anyone can verify. Your contacts are deans, heads of department, directors of studies, IT managers and finance directors. A typical decision involves three to five people and takes between two and four months.

You are responsible for the whole sales cycle. You identify institutions that fit, get the first meeting, understand how they teach and assess today, present StudyVibe with a live demonstration, prepare the commercial proposal, negotiate, and obtain the signature. After the signature you stay involved until the institution has run its first evaluation; our customer-success team takes over the onboarding itself.

In your first month you and the head of sales agree, in writing, your targets for signed institutions and for students enrolled on the platform per quarter. You update your pipeline in the CRM every Friday, and the head of sales reviews it with you every week. Your success is measured by signed institutions, by students enrolled on the platform, and by how accurate your forecast is.

This is a hybrid position based in Douala. You are in our Douala office two days a week for pipeline reviews and team work. The rest of your time is spent visiting institutions, mostly in Douala and Yaoundé. You should expect to travel to other cities and to neighbouring countries about one week per month; StudyVibe pays the travel costs. The contract is full-time.

## Responsibilities

- Build and keep up to date a list of target institutions in Cameroon and the region, with the names of the people who decide.
- Make first contact by phone, e-mail and visits, and obtain meetings with the people who decide.
- Run discovery meetings to understand how the institution teaches, evaluates and certifies today, and what problem it wants to solve.
- Deliver StudyVibe demonstrations, online and in person, in French or English, adapted to the audience.
- Prepare commercial proposals and quotes, and negotiate price and terms with the institution's management, finance and legal contacts.
- Answer calls for tenders and requests for proposals, with the help of the product and operations teams.
- Keep the CRM accurate: every contact, meeting, proposal and next step is recorded within one working day.
- Hand each signed institution over to the customer-success team with a written summary of what was promised and what the institution expects.
- Give the product team structured feedback from the field: what institutions ask for, what makes them hesitate, what competitors offer.
- Represent StudyVibe at education events, conferences and university meetings.
- Report your pipeline and your forecast every week.

## Requirements

- At least 3 years of experience in B2B sales or business development, with a record of closed deals that you can describe: what you sold, to whom, for what value, and how long it took.
- Experience selling software, online services or training to universities, schools, training providers, public institutions or large companies where several people take the decision.
- The ability to run a structured sales process: qualification, discovery, demonstration, proposal, negotiation and close.
- Excellent spoken and written French and English: you can present to a dean in either language.
- Confidence in front of an audience: you can run a one-hour demonstration online and in person.
- Working knowledge of a CRM (for example HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce or Pipedrive) and of spreadsheets for forecasting.
- Willingness to travel within Cameroon and abroad about one week per month, with a valid passport.
- A bachelor's degree in business, commerce, communication or a related field, or equivalent professional experience that you describe in your application.
- The right to work in Cameroon.

## Nice to have

- Existing relationships with universities or training centres in Central or West Africa.
- Experience selling education technology, a learning management system or another software-as-a-service product.
- Experience answering public or private tenders.
- Experience in higher education as a lecturer or an administrator.
- Knowledge of accreditation and quality-assurance processes in higher education.

- A fixed salary plus a performance bonus linked to signed institutions and enrolled students; the exact structure is shared with shortlisted candidates before the first interview.
- Travel costs paid by StudyVibe.

- Training on the product and on the education market during your first weeks, with direct access to the people who build StudyVibe.
- A laptop and the tools you need to do the job.
